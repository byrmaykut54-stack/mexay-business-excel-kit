# MexAy Business Excel Kit

## Run your small business from one professional Excel workbook

Stop juggling separate notes, spreadsheets and scattered records. MexAy Business Excel Kit brings **appointments, customers, income, expenses and business performance** into one ready-to-use workbook.

### What you get
- Professional business Dashboard
- Appointment tracker with status dropdowns
- Customer database
- Income and expense tracker
- Automatic revenue, expense and net-earnings summaries
- Appointment status chart
- Filters and frozen headers for easier navigation
- Settings page for business details and working hours
- Quick Start page
- PDF user guide
- 3 promotional visuals
- Single-business license

### Built for
Barbers, hairdressers, beauty salons, consultants, trainers, tutors, freelancers and other appointment-based small businesses.

### How it works
1. Open the Excel workbook.
2. Enter your business information in **Ayarlar / Settings**.
3. Add customers.
4. Record appointments.
5. Enter income and expenses.
6. Use the Dashboard to monitor your business at a glance.

### Important
This is a digital Excel template, not a cloud appointment-management service. Microsoft Excel is required to edit and use the workbook. Each input sheet supports 500 records. All financial values are TRY. Appointment fees are not automatically booked as income; actual transactions are entered in the Finance sheet. Customer visit/spend totals are entered manually. Business-hour settings are informational. The workbook starts empty.
